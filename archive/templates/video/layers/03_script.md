# Layer 3 — Script

**What it is:** The production document. Frame-by-frame structure with VO and visual brief per frame, built on the confirmed concept. Defines the number of frames, timing, exact VO lines, and visual intent.

**What it does not do:** Specify image generation parameters, camera movement, or motion technique. Those belong to the Visuals and Motion layers.

**The critical rule:** The visual track and VO track are written separately and operate independently. They meet in meaning, not in description. The image carries the metaphor; the VO carries the business story. Neither explains the other.

**The test:** Does timing across all frames add to the target duration? Does every frame have a confirmed VO line or a deliberate notation of silence? Could someone read only the VO track and follow the business story? Could someone watch only the visual track and follow the metaphor?

**Governance:** Confirmed before any images are generated. No shot is specified until the script frame it belongs to is locked.

**File naming:** `SCRIPT.md`

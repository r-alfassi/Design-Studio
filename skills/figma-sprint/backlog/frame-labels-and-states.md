# F-005 - Label every frame on the canvas with a name and a state

- **Status:** open
- **Observed:** 2026-10-19, reading the sprint pages of two personal projects.
  One page showed three variants of a screen at the same size with no frame
  name or state visible on the canvas, so baseline and current authority could
  not be told apart without the layer panel. Another dimmed its rejected
  frames and suffixed their names, but the standing was easy to miss. A third
  case left a sprint's Final outcome in a neighbouring unnamed section.
- **Operating correction:** put a name and one state label (current, proposal,
  rejected, superseded, final outcome, placeholder) on every frame on the
  canvas; label rejected branches in words and keep them beside the work they
  were compared with; mark gaps with a placeholder box rather than invented
  content; leave no unnamed section on a sprint page.
- **Close when:** the next completed sprint on a page built with the Design
  Studio components passes a canvas read-through in which every frame's state
  can be named without opening the layer panel, and the hierarchy review finds
  no unnamed section.

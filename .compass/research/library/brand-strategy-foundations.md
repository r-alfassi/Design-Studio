## Why this shelf exists

These sources informed the Design Studio-authored
[`frameworks/brand-strategy-foundations.md`](../frameworks/brand-strategy-foundations.md).
They are kept as attributed pointers, not copied methodology. They may inform a
bounded application, but neither the sources nor this shelf govern a project.

| Source | Owner | Useful contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Value Proposition Canvas](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-value-proposition-canvas) | Strategyzer | A way to distinguish an audience's jobs, pains, and gains from an offering's claimed value. |
| [The Golden Circle](https://simonsinek.com/golden-circle) | The Optimism Company / Simon Sinek | A purpose-first prompt that separates why, how, and what. |
| [Crafting a Positioning Statement](https://standpointgroup.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/How-to-Write-a-Positioning-Statement.pdf) | StandPoint Group | A positioning structure linking target audience, frame, benefit/difference, and reason to believe. |
| [Brand Identity Prism](https://www.warc.com/en/article/brand-identity-prism-3f51d423f2b747bb9c575814bfdacc9c) | Jean-Noël Kapferer / WARC | A reminder that brand identity spans expression, personality, values, relationships, and audience self-image. |

## Use boundary

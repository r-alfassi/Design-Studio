# Audience

This file says who the project serves, what each of them is like, and what they need.

## The Designer

### Who they are

The person who approves material changes to Design Studio and makes the project-specific design decisions that its residents must not make.

### What they need

- To know that reusable material is separate from client or project-specific content.
- To find the relevant craft without maintaining several copies of it.
- To see which proposed resident still needs a decision before it becomes guidance.

## Principal and project agents

### Who they are

Agents working on a project that needs a bounded design procedure, format, framework, standard, or reference.

### What they need

- To find a relevant resident and understand its status before applying it.
- To apply reusable craft without treating it as a project decision or implementation authority.
- To know where a change to reusable material is recorded.

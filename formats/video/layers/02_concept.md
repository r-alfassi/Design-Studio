# Layer 2 — Concept

**What it is:** The creative translation of the narrative — metaphor, semantic field, and beat-by-beat mapping from story to visual moment. This is where the visual world is chosen and the logic of the metaphor is established.

**What it does not do:** Write VO lines, specify shots, or make execution decisions. The concept describes what the visual world *means*, not what is *in frame*.

**The test:** Can a skeptic follow the logic of why this metaphor was chosen, without seeing an image? Does every narrative beat have a visual equivalent that can be explained in plain language?

**Governance:** Confirmed before the script is written. Present at the level of a productive client conversation — not a finished spec. Over-specifying before the client has reacted closes down the discussion.

**Core sections:**
- The metaphor — stated plainly
- The semantic field — what objects, actions, and settings belong to this world, and what each represents
- Beat-by-beat mapping — narrative beat → visual moment → emotional register
- Cinematic reference — the visual tone and pacing anchor
- Visual world constants — light, color, camera, setting

**File naming:** `CONCEPT.md`

# Layer 1 — Narrative

**What it is:** The plain business story — what we want to say, with no creative device applied. Written as if explaining to a colleague. Situation, arc, what the audience needs to leave believing.

**What it does not do:** Use metaphor, visual language, or semantic field. If it needs a visual to be understood, it has not been separated from the concept yet.

**The test:** Can the story be stated in 3–5 sentences? Would a client recognize it as accurate? Does it make sense with no image attached?

**Governance:** Confirmed before the concept is written. Changes to the narrative require revisiting the concept.

**File naming:** `NARRATIVE.md`

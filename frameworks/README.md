# Frameworks — resident index

A framework is a **thinking method** — a sequence of questions and quality bars
for reasoning to a design choice. It prescribes no deliverable shape and no
procedure; the project picks its own medium and output. See `../STRUCTURE.md`.

| Framework | Reach for it when… |
|---|---|
| `brand-strategy-foundations.md` | a product, service, or personal brand needs its strategic basis made explicit before expression is treated as settled — audience, purpose, positioning, proof, values |
| `which-way-in.md` | visual work starts and the request does not name its way in: a sprint in Figma, formalizing a component, or tuning in place |
| `which-side-is-newer.md` | a component exists in both Figma and the product and the direction is needed: before formalizing, or when writing a design-debt row |
| `ux-expert.md` | reasoning toward a UX judgment against the 30 Laws of UX — critique, prioritization, explaining why a pattern works |

Each framework may have a source shelf in `../library/` (attributed external
material that informed it — non-binding).

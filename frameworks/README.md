# Frameworks — resident index

A framework is a **thinking method** — a sequence of questions and quality bars
for reasoning to a design choice. It prescribes no deliverable shape and no
procedure; the project picks its own medium and output. See `../STRUCTURE.md`.

| Framework | Reach for it when… |
|---|---|
| `is-the-diagram-done.md` | a diagram has been drawn, or is about to be, on a FigJam board and it must be where the Designer asked and readable: before drawing and before calling it done. Drafted 2026-11-04, not yet routed from the spine or a skill (Q-69) |
| `which-side-is-newer.md` | a component exists in both Figma and the product and the direction is needed: before formalizing, or when writing a design-debt row |
